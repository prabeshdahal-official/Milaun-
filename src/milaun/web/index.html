Milaun मिलाउनु

Decide whether two Nepali names describe the same person, even when one is written in Devanagari and the other in Latin script. Runs entirely on this machine.

Compare two names

Try a pair such as प्रवेश दाहाल and Prabesh Dahal, or Bhattarai and Bhattarai.

Find duplicates in a list

One record per line as id, name. Ids are optional and are generated when omitted. Nothing leaves the machine.

Milaun returns a probability, not a verdict. Pairs in the review band are the ones worth a human decision: automatic merging is reserved for high-confidence matches, and every score can be inspected feature by feature.

Milaun is open source (MIT). The phonetic key was designed for Nepali orthography and romanisation habits; it is not a general-purpose transliterator. Scores are calibrated on a synthetic benchmark, so calibrate again on your own data before automatic merging.
